HYPIUM OPEN ENGINEERING

Our engineering process is open.

From problem to prototype and from testing to results: an engineering log where Hypium transparently documents technical decisions and lessons learned.

01 / ENGINEERING PROCESS

Before the result
we show the decisions.

Open Engineering is designed to document not only what Hypium builds, but why a solution was chosen, how it was tested and what was learned from failures.

01

Problem

Define game requirements and real-world constraints.

02

Design

Generate alternatives, define criteria and move the selected ideas into CAD.

03

Prototype

Test the most critical assumptions with rapid prototypes.

04

Test

Collect and compare measurable performance data.

05

Result

Clearly record the decision, result and next iteration.
