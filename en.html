01 / ABOUT US

How does a dragon
build a robot?

Hypium is a student engineering community founded at Düzce Science High School and working for the FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) For us, robotics is more than a competition; it is an environment where we learn to solve problems, build, research and turn ideas into reality as a team.

Our 40 students take responsibility across mechanics, electronics, software, CAD, media and business development. Each subteam develops its expertise while working toward the same goal: building a reliable, competitive and well-engineered robot.

We complement our workshop skills with communication, leadership, budgeting, sponsorship and social-impact work. Hypium aims to prepare its members not only for competition, but for their future journeys in engineering and technology.

02 / WHAT IS FRC?

Six weeks, one robot,
endless possibilities.

FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC), dünyanın farklı bölgelerinden lise öğrencilerini mühendislik, yazılım, tasarım, strateji ve takım çalışması etrafında buluşturan uluslararası bir robotik programıdır. FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) çatısı altında yürütülen program, öğrencilerin gerçek bir mühendislik ekibine benzer şekilde çalışmasını hedefler.

Each season introduces a new game and field challenge. Teams analyze the game, define the robot architecture, prototype mechanisms, build mechanical and electrical systems, write software and perform drive tests. At competitions, teams play in three-team alliances, demonstrating both technical performance and team culture.

01
Kickoff

The season game is announced and teams begin developing strategy.

02
Build Season

The robot is designed, built and tested during the build season.

03
Regional Competitions

Teams form alliances and compete on the field.

04
Championship

Top-performing teams advance toward the world championship.

FRC is more than a robotics competition; "Gracious Professionalism" ve "Coopertition" it is an experience that combines engineering, teamwork and sportsmanship through its core values.

02.1 / HOW WE WORK

From an idea to the field.

An FRC robot is never the product of a single department. Throughout the season, decisions in strategy, design, manufacturing and software affect one another. At Hypium, our subteams work as connected parts of one engineering process.

We first analyze the game requirements and identify the tasks that create the most value. We then test ideas with rapid prototypes and move successful solutions into CAD, manufacturing and software. Once systems come together, intensive testing, debugging and drive practice begin.

Our goal is not simply to make a working robot, but to make data-informed decisions, divide responsibilities effectively and leave a stronger team foundation after every season.

SEASON FLOW
  • 01
    Game Analysis

    Rules, scoring and field tasks are analyzed.

  • 02
    Strategy

    Robot priorities and match approach are defined.

  • 03
    Prototyping

    Mechanism ideas are validated through rapid testing.

  • 04
    Design & Manufacturing

    CAD, mechanical and electrical systems are integrated.

  • 05
    Software & Testing

    Control, autonomous and sensor systems are tested on the field.

  • 06
    Competition

    The robot, drive team and strategy come together in real matches.

03 / OUR STORY

From Düzce
to the world.

Hypium FRC, in April 2026 Düzce Fen Lisesi çatısı altında resmi olarak kuruldu. Düzce'nin first and only FRC team olarak yola çıkarken hedefimiz yalnızca bir yarışmaya katılmak değil; şehrimizde kalıcı bir mühendislik, üretim ve STEM kültürü oluşturmaktı.

Hypium takes its name from the ancient heritage in Konuralp, Düzce: Prusias ad Hypium Our name draws inspiration from the history of our city, carrying that heritage into the future through technology and engineering.
